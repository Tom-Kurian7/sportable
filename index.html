What happens next

  1. We reply within a week to confirm the item and arrange drop-off or pickup.
  2. A partner technician checks safety, cleans it and records the size.
  3. It joins the lending library and goes to a player on the waitlist.
SportAble Calgary is a student concept for the 2026 Experience Ventures Hackathon. We aim to work with Calgary Adapted Hub, Wheelchair Sports Alberta, WinSport and local clubs, not replace them.
